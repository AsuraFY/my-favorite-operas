# Das Rheingold — four-scene German-English working libretto

**Status:** Complete working text integrated on `Update`. Not yet a human-edited or singable English translation. The `main` branch remains unchanged.

## Source and structure

Original: Richard Wagner, *Das Rheingold* (1869), public domain. German source: [Opera Guide](https://opera-guide.ch/operas/das+rheingold/libretto/de/) via [Sottovoce Q327717](https://github.com/martin-he543/sottovoce/blob/ff573f9e35a565bdb6afed35c83f091e69f5a40b/public/libretti/texts/Q327717.json), also cross-checkable via [Naxos libretto](https://cdn.naxos.com/sharedfiles/sungtext/libretti/wagner/das_rheingold/scene_i.htm).

One continuous prologue act divided into four scenes:

| Scene | Source records |
|---|---:|
| Erste Szene | 286 |
| Zweite Szene | 489 |
| Dritte Szene | 322 |
| Vierte Szene | 484 |
| **Total** | **1,581** |

The source has 1,312 sung and 269 stage-direction records. Each retains its original text and is paired with an English working line (including scenic action). Identical consecutive speaker passages are grouped in the reading view; subsection labels are original incipits rather than invented musical-number titles. Four authentic scene divisions remain intact.

## Editorial limitations

English was produced with machine assistance, with two opening Rhine-maiden lines adjusted by hand. Musical sound-words, poetry, alliteration, wordplay, archaic grammar, nuance and Wagnerian names require human review. Do not promote as a faithful performance-ready libretto until checked against the original and a literary translation. Native German stage directions and all source line records remain available in the bilingual text.
