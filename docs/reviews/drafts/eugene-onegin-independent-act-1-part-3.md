# Eugene Onegin — Independent English translation draft
## Act I · Tableau 3 · No. 11 and closing scene

**Status:** first-pass English translation from [Russian Act I, tableau 3 (Wikisource/FEB)](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Евгений_Онегин_(опера)/Действие_первое); not editorially approved or deployed. This historical source marks **No. 11 — Girls' chorus**, followed by the scene with Onegin, rather than a separately numbered No. 12.

### Tableau 3 — A secluded part of the Larins' garden

*Young servant girls pick berries and sing together.*

### No. 11 — Girls' chorus

**GIRLS**

Young maidens, lovely maidens,
dearest friends and companions!
Laugh and play, you girls,
make merry, my dear ones!
Raise a song together,
a song we know by heart.
Entice a young man here,
draw him into our dancing circle!
Once he has been lured to us,
once we see him in the distance,
we shall scatter, dear friends,
showering him with cherries,
cherries, raspberries,
and bright red currants!
Do not come to overhear
our secret songs;
do not come spying on
the games we girls are playing!

*The girls withdraw farther into the garden. Tatyana runs in, visibly distressed, and sinks exhausted onto a bench.*

**TATYANA**

He is here! Eugene is here!
Oh heavens! What must he be thinking?
What will he say?
Why—why did I give way
to the cries of my troubled heart,
why couldn't I control myself?
Why did I write that letter to him?
My heart tells me now
that the man who has bewitched me
will only laugh at me.
Oh God, how unhappy I am!
How utterly wretched!

*She hears approaching footsteps and listens.*

Footsteps—they are coming closer.
Yes, it is he! It is he!

*Onegin appears.*

### Closing scene — Onegin's reply

**ONEGIN**

You wrote to me.
Do not deny it. I have read
the confession of a trusting soul,
the outpouring of innocent love.
Your sincerity is precious to me.
It stirred feelings that had long lain silent.
But I shall not offer you mere praise.
I shall repay your candour
with an equally plain confession.
Listen to what I have to say,
and be my judge.

**TATYANA (aside)**

Oh God! How humiliating, how painful!

**ONEGIN**

If I had wished to confine my life
within the circle of a family,
if fate had called me
to the pleasant lot of father and husband,
then surely I would have sought no bride
other than you.

But happiness of that sort is not for me;
my soul is a stranger to it.
Your virtues are wasted on me;
I am not worthy of them.
Believe me—my conscience gives its word—
marriage would be torment for us both.

However deeply I might love you,
once accustomed to you, my love would vanish.
Consider what kind of joys
the god of marriage would have prepared for us,
perhaps for many years to come!
Dreams and youth will never return;
there is no going back.
I cannot renew my soul.

I love you with a brother's affection,
as a brother loves,
perhaps with something even more tender—
yes, perhaps more tender still.
Listen to me without resentment:
a young girl will change her fleeting dreams
for other dreams more than once.

**GIRLS (offstage)**

Lovely maidens, dearest friends!
Laugh and play, you girls; make merry!

**ONEGIN**

Learn to govern your feelings.
Not everyone would understand you as I do;
inexperience can lead to disaster.

*Onegin offers Tatyana his arm, and they walk towards the house. The girls continue their song as they move away.*

**GIRLS (in the distance)**

Once we have lured a young man here,
once we see him in the distance,
we shall scatter and shower him with cherries.
Do not come to overhear,
do not come spying on
the games we girls are playing!
