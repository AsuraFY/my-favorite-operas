# Eugene Onegin — Independent English translation draft
## Act II · Tableau 4 · Nos. 13–16

**Status:** first-pass independent English translation from [Russian Act II, tableau 4 (Wikisource/FEB)](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Евгений_Онегин_(опера)/Действие_второе). Not editorially approved and not displayed on the website. This Russian edition labels the first number of Act II as **No. 13**, following the source's numbering convention.

### Tableau 4 — Tatyana’s name-day ball at the Larins’ house

*Young people dance. Older guests sit in groups talking and watching the dancing.*

### No. 13 — Entr'acte, waltz, scene and chorus

**GUESTS**

What a surprise! We never expected
a military band here!
What splendid entertainment!
It has been so long since we were treated so well.
A marvellous celebration, wouldn't you say?
So long since we've had such a treat!
A wonderful celebration, wouldn't you say?
Bravo, bravo, bravo, bravo!
What a surprise for us!
Bravo, bravo, bravo, bravo!
What a splendid surprise!

**OLDER LANDOWNERS**

At our country estates we seldom see
the sparkling joy of a merry ball.
Hunting is our only pastime,
and we love the noise and excitement of the chase.

**OLDER LADIES**

Some entertainment! They rush all day
through thickets, meadows, marshes and bushes,
wear themselves out, then lie down to rest—
and that's the pleasure left to us poor women!

*A captain appears. The young women gather around him.*

**YOUNG WOMEN**

Oh, Trifon Petrovich,
how very kind of you!
We are so grateful!

**CAPTAIN**

Oh, please!
The pleasure is entirely mine!

**YOUNG WOMEN**

We shall have a wonderful dance!

**CAPTAIN**

And I mean to enjoy it myself.
Come, let us begin!

*The dancing resumes. Tatyana and Onegin are among the couples, attracting the ladies' attention.*

**FIRST GROUP OF LADIES**

Just look! Just look!
There go the two lovebirds!

**SECOND GROUP**

About time, too!

**FIRST GROUP**

What a bridegroom!

**SECOND GROUP**

Poor little Tanya!

**FIRST GROUP**

He'll marry her ...

**BOTH GROUPS**

And then he'll tyrannize her!
They say he is a gambler!

*When the dance ends, Onegin walks slowly through the room, overhearing the conversation.*

**LADIES**

He is shockingly ill-mannered, madly eccentric;
he will not even kiss a lady's hand.
He is a Freemason, and drinks nothing
but red wine by the glass!

**ONEGIN (aside)**

So that is their opinion!
I have heard quite enough
of this disgusting gossip.
And I have only myself to blame!
Why did I come to this foolish ball?
I shall never forgive Vladimir
for doing me this favour.
I shall pay attention to Olga
and make him thoroughly furious.
There she is!

*Onegin approaches Olga, just as Lensky comes towards her.*

**ONEGIN (to Olga)**

May I have this dance?

**LENSKY (to Olga)**

You promised it to me!

**ONEGIN (to Lensky)**

You must have misunderstood.

*Olga dances with Onegin.*

**LENSKY (aside)**

What is happening?
I cannot believe my eyes! Olga!
Heavens, what has come over me?

**GUESTS**

What a glorious feast! What a surprise!
What fine hospitality!
Such wonderful entertainment!
A glorious feast! What a surprise!
Who expected a military band?
What entertainment!
It's been so long since we've had such a treat!
A magnificent feast, isn't it?
Bravo, bravo, bravo, bravo!
What a surprise for us!
Bravo, bravo, bravo, bravo!
A wonderful feast, don't you agree?
Yes, a military band was wholly unexpected!
A splendid feast! Such merriment!
A splendid feast!

*As Olga finishes her dance, Lensky approaches. Onegin watches from a distance.*

### No. 14 — Scene and Triquet’s couplets

**LENSKY (to Olga)**

Have I deserved to be mocked like this?
Oh, Olga, how cruelly you treat me!
What have I done?

**OLGA**

I have no idea what wrong I've done!

**LENSKY**

You danced every écossaise, every waltz
with Onegin!
I asked you, and you refused me.

**OLGA**

Vladimir, how strange you are!
You get angry over trifles.

**LENSKY**

Trifles?
Was I supposed to watch without feeling
while you laughed and flirted with him?
He leaned towards you, pressed your hand—
I saw it all!

**OLGA**

It's nonsense, every word!
You are jealous for no reason.
We were merely chatting.
He is so agreeable!

**LENSKY**

Agreeable, is he?
Oh, Olga, you do not love me!

**OLGA**

How strangely you're behaving!

**LENSKY**

You do not love me!
Will you dance the cotillion with me?

**ONEGIN**

No, with me.
You gave me your word, did you not?

**OLGA (to Onegin)**

And I shall keep my word.

*Lensky gestures imploringly.*

**OLGA (to Lensky)**

There is your punishment
for being so jealous!

**LENSKY**

Olga!

**OLGA**

I will not change my mind!

*Olga and Onegin turn away. A lively group of young ladies comes towards them.*

**OLGA**

Look! All the young ladies
are coming this way with Triquet.

**ONEGIN**

Who is he?

**OLGA**

A Frenchman who lives with Kharlikov.

**YOUNG WOMEN**

Monsieur Triquet, Monsieur Triquet!
Be so kind as to sing a verse!

**TRIQUET (in broken Russian and French)**

I have brought a song with me;
but where is the young lady?
She must stand before me,
for this song was composed for her!

**YOUNG WOMEN**

There she is! There she is!

**TRIQUET**

Ah, so here you are!
Here is the queen of the day.
Ladies, I am about to begin.
Please, do not interrupt me.

*Triquet ceremoniously brings Tatyana into the middle of the circle and sings.*

**TRIQUET (his French verses, rendered into English)**

Gathered for this celebration,
let us admire the charm and beauty
of the young lady whose birthday we honour.
Her gentle, enchanting presence
sheds its brightness over us all.
What pleasure, what happiness to behold her!
May destiny fulfil her every wish;
may joy, games and delight
always keep a smile upon her lips.
May she be like a star that shines forever
in the sky above this country,
lighting our days and our nights.
You are a rose, you are a rose,
a lovely rose, beautiful Tatyana!
You are a rose, you are a rose,
a lovely rose, beautiful Tatyana!

**TRIQUET (in broken Russian)**

What a wonderful day it was
when beautiful Tatyana
was born here in this quiet countryside!
And we have come here—
girls and ladies, gentlemen too—
to watch her blossom like a flower!

**GUESTS**

Bravo, bravo, bravo, Monsieur Triquet!
Your song is splendid,
and so charmingly sung!

**TRIQUET (in broken Russian with French phrases)**

May you enjoy great happiness,
remain forever the fairy of these shores,
and never be bored or ill.
And amid all your good fortune,
do not forget your humble servant
or all your friends!
You are a rose, you are a rose,
beautiful Tatyana!
You are a rose, you are a rose,
beautiful Tatyana!

**GUESTS**

Bravo, bravo, bravo, Monsieur Triquet!
Your song is splendid
and so charmingly sung!

### No. 15 — Mazurka and scene

**CAPTAIN**

Ladies and gentlemen, take your places, please!
The cotillion is about to begin!
Come along!

*A mazurka begins. Onegin dances with Olga while Lensky watches jealously. When the dance ends, Onegin approaches Lensky.*

**ONEGIN**

Not dancing, Lensky?
You stand there like Childe Harold.
What's the matter with you?

**LENSKY**

With me? Nothing.
I am admiring you,
what a wonderful friend you are!

**ONEGIN**

Well, well!
I never expected such a compliment.
Why are you sulking?

**LENSKY**

Sulking? Not at all!
I am admiring the way
your clever words and fashionable chatter
turn girls' heads
and disturb their peace of mind.
Tatyana alone is evidently not enough for you.
For love of me, you surely mean
to ruin Olga's happiness,
upset her heart, and then laugh at her.
What honourable conduct!

**ONEGIN**

What? You must have lost your mind!

**LENSKY**

Wonderful!
You insult me
and then call me insane.

**GUESTS (surrounding them)**

What's happening? What is it?
What are they arguing about?

**LENSKY**

Onegin, you are no longer my friend!
I want nothing more to do with you.
I despise you!

**GUESTS**

What an unexpected surprise!
What a quarrel has broken out!
This has become serious!

**ONEGIN (taking Lensky aside)**

Listen, Lensky, you are mistaken.
You're mistaken!
We've attracted enough attention
with this argument.
I have disturbed no one's peace,
and I assure you I have no intention
of doing so.

**LENSKY**

Then why did you squeeze her hand,
why whisper things to her?
She blushed and laughed!
What were you saying to her?

**ONEGIN**

Listen, this is absurd!
Everyone is watching us.

**LENSKY**

I do not care!
You have insulted me.
I demand satisfaction.

**GUESTS**

What is it?
Tell us, tell us what has happened!

**LENSKY**

Quite simply, I demand
that Mr Onegin explain his actions to me.
He refuses. Therefore
I ask him to accept my challenge.

**LARINA**

Oh, heavens! In my own house!
Have mercy, please!

### No. 16 — Finale

**LENSKY**

In your house! In your house!
Here in your home,
my childhood passed like a golden dream.
Here I first learned
the joy of innocent, radiant love.
But today I have discovered something else:
I have learned that life is no romance;
honour is only an empty sound,
friendship a hollow word,
a cruel and pitiful deception!

**ONEGIN (aside)**

Alone before my conscience,
I am dissatisfied with myself.
I have mocked his shy, tender love
far too carelessly.
Loving that young man with all my heart,
I should have shown myself
not a captive of prejudice,
but a man of honour and sound judgment.

**TATYANA (aside)**

I am shaken; my mind cannot
understand Eugene's conduct.
A jealous anguish troubles me,
torments me!
Like someone's icy hand
it grips my heart—
so painfully, so cruelly.

**LARINA AND OLGA (aside)**

I fear that after all this merriment
the night may end in a duel!

**GUESTS**

Poor Lensky! Poor young man!

**ONEGIN**

I have made a cruel, careless joke.

**LENSKY**

Here I have learned that a maiden
can be as beautiful and lovely as an angel,
radiant as the day,
while her soul—her very soul—
can be treacherous and wicked as a demon!

**TATYANA (aside)**

Ah, I am lost, I am lost!
My heart tells me so.
Yet even ruin at his hands is dear to me.
I shall perish, my heart has said,
and I dare not complain.
Why should I complain?
He cannot make me happy!

**OLGA (aside)**

Men are so hot-blooded:
they act without thinking.
They cannot get along without a quarrel!
Jealousy has taken hold of his heart,
but I have done nothing wrong!

**LARINA (aside)**

Young people are so hot-blooded!
They rush to conclusions
and cannot be without a quarrel.
After all this merriment,
I fear the night will end in a duel!

**OLGA AND LARINA (each aside)**

They argue, they quarrel,
and at once they are ready to fight.
What a fine celebration!
What a scandal!

**ONEGIN (aside)**

Alone before my conscience,
I am dissatisfied with myself.
I have mocked his shy, tender love
far too carelessly.
I ought to have shown myself
not a plaything of prejudice,
but a man grown wise and mature.
I am to blame!
Alone before my conscience
I am dissatisfied with myself.
I have made a careless joke
of his shy and tender passion.
But nothing can be done:
I must now answer for the insults.

**GUESTS**

Will their quarrel follow our entertainment
and end in a duel?
Men are so hot-blooded,
acting rashly in everything.
They cannot avoid an argument;
now they are ready to fight!
What a fine celebration!
What a scandal!

**LENSKY**

No, Olga, you are innocent, my angel.
You are innocent, entirely innocent.
He is a base, deceitful, heartless betrayer;
he shall be punished!
You are innocent, my angel.
He is your ignoble tempter,
but I shall be your defender.
I will never endure a seducer
tempting your young heart
with heated sighs and praise;
nor a vile and poisonous worm
gnawing at the stem of a lily
until the flower, barely open,
wilts before its second dawn.
Oh, traitor! Dishonourable seducer!

**ONEGIN (approaching Lensky)**

At your service!
I have heard quite enough.
You are mad, utterly mad!
This lesson may bring you to your senses.

**LENSKY**

Then we shall meet tomorrow
and see who teaches whom!
Call me mad if you like,
but you are a dishonourable seducer!

**ONEGIN**

Be silent, or I shall kill you!

**GUESTS**

What a scandal!
We shall never permit this duel.
We simply must keep them from leaving the house!
Hold them back, hold them back!
We won't let them go!

**OLGA**

Vladimir, calm yourself, I beg you!

**LENSKY**

Ah, Olga! Olga!
Farewell for ever!

*He hurries away.*

**GUESTS**

There will be a duel!
